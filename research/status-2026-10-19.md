2. **Measured parallel throughput.** The new runner supports 1–8 workers with a shared rate limiter, 429 backoff and run-wide stop conditions, but only synthetic tests exist; no live multi-worker measurement.
3. **Memo stage for scaled runs.** The pilot memo is tied to the pilot ledger. A scaled memo adapter (reusing `memo.py` unchanged, separate ledger, `OPENAI_API_KEY`) is not written yet. Model choice is pending: keep measured `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` or measure GPT-6 Luna. No `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` exists in `.env`, so Haiku would need a new key from Tito's Anthropic Console.

5. **Deployed database, backend and dashboard.** Not started.
6. **Screen recording** of interruption and resume. Outstanding; the 500 checkpoint includes a planned interruption that can be recorded.
7. **Human golden labels and evaluation** against them.
8. **Credit balances** not verified: no non-billable balance check was available from the project. Tito must confirm in the TypeSafe and OpenAI consoles which account holds the expiring credits.
